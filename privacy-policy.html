SENDS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how SENDS, the climbing game for Android (com.sends.climbing), handles information. SENDS is provided by the developer identified in its Google Play listing ("we", "us").

The Google Play version works offline, requires no SENDS account, and keeps game saves on your device. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not automatically upload your gameplay, saves, or device identifiers to us.

1. Information stored on your device

SENDS uses local app storage to save:

  • Completed climbs, best scores, action counts, completion dates, and other saved progress.
  • Your climb ratings, saved custom or generated climbs, and imported climb settings.
  • Names, setter names, descriptions, and other climb details you choose to enter.
  • Preferences such as difficulty, rendering quality, leg flexibility, movement mode, and camera controls.

This information lets the game remember your progress, settings, and climbs between sessions. It is stored in the Android app's local WebView storage, or in your browser's local storage when playing a web version. We do not receive these saved records through ordinary use of the Google Play version.

The character's body coordinates, strain, power, and flexibility values describe a fictional game character. SENDS does not measure your real body, fitness, or health.

2. Automatic collection, advertising, and permissions

The Google Play version has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or automatic crash-reporting service. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, camera images, advertising ID, or persistent hardware identifiers, and it does not request access to these resources.

The Android app declares the Internet permission, but the game, included climbs, and climb workshop are bundled with the app and work offline. The permission does not mean that SENDS automatically uploads your game data.

SENDS does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. Local storage is used for game functionality as described above.

3. Sharing and importing climbs

When you choose Copy string, SENDS writes that climb's share code to your device's clipboard. The code includes its name, grade, setter, description, hold roles, and foot rules. It does not include your ascent records or personal climb ratings.

You decide whether to send that code to someone else using another app. The recipient and the service you use to send it can receive the information in the code. SENDS does not upload it to a climb-sharing server. Your device or keyboard may also provide clipboard history or clipboard syncing according to its own settings.

Importing reads a code you paste into the import form and saves the resulting climb locally only when you choose to save it. SENDS does not automatically read your clipboard in the background.

4. Information you send to app support

If you contact us at sendsclimbing@gmail.com, we receive your email address or other contact details and the contents of your message, including attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate problems, and handle your request.

Support correspondence is processed using Gmail, provided by Google. We retain it for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any related legal obligations. You can request deletion using the contact method below.

5. Other services and device backups

We do not sell your personal information or provide your gameplay records to advertisers or analytics companies.

Google Play, Android, your device manufacturer, and any service you choose to use for sharing or support operate under their own privacy policies. They may process information independently of SENDS, for example when you download an app, use device diagnostics, or send a support email.

Depending on your Android version and device settings, the operating system or your device provider may back up and restore app data. SENDS does not operate its own cloud-save service. Manage system backups and any backup copies through your device or backup provider's settings. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When you visit a publicly hosted copy of this policy, your browser contacts that website's hosting service. The host may process standard request information, such as your IP address, browser information, and request time, to deliver and secure the page. This policy page contains no analytics scripts, third-party fonts, or tracking images.

6. Retention and deletion of game data

Saved game information remains on your device until it is changed, overwritten, or removed. There is no SENDS account or server-side player profile to delete.

  • Android: open your device's Settings, find SENDS under Apps, and use Storage & cache → Clear storage (wording varies by device). Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.
  • Web version: use your browser's site-data settings to delete the stored data for the website where you play SENDS.
  • Backups and shared copies: manage operating-system backups separately. Clearing SENDS cannot remove codes already sent to other people or copied into other apps.

Clearing local storage removes your saved progress, ratings, custom climbs, and preferences. We cannot recover records that exist only on your device.

7. Data security

Local game records rely on the storage protections supplied by Android or your browser. SENDS does not add a separate encryption layer to those records. Keeping your device and browser secure helps protect them. We do not maintain an online database of players' local game saves.

If we receive support information, we use it for the request and limit access to the people and service providers involved in handling it. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

The Google Play version does not ask for a player's age or require personal information to play. It has no targeted advertising or player tracking, including for children. A parent or guardian can remove locally saved information using the steps above and can contact us about information voluntarily sent to support.

9. Development-server testing

The following applies only when playing a development-server version, not the packaged Google Play release.

Development testing automatically sends diagnostic play logs to that development server. These include a randomly generated session identifier, timestamps, app and browser information, screen dimensions, route details, game-character positions, settings, actions, planner messages, and predicted versus actual movement outcomes.

If you use the development-only Log issue form, the server also receives your comment and a snapshot of the game state, including pose coordinates, holds, messages, camera settings, and recent diagnostic events. These records are used to investigate bugs and improve the game. They remain on the development server until manually deleted by the developer; you can request deletion through the contact below, quoting the session identifier or report date where available.

Automatic development logging and the issue-submission form are disabled in production builds, including Android packages built from the production game.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies this version. Any new data practices will be described in the updated policy and, where needed, within the app.

11. Privacy contact

For privacy questions, support-data deletion requests, or other privacy enquiries, email sendsclimbing@gmail.com. Please identify the app as SENDS and include "Privacy" in your enquiry so it can be directed appropriately.

This is also the contact for a parent or guardian's privacy request. For game records stored only on your device, use the local deletion instructions in section 6.
